Our story

Four chairs, two brothers, one good shop

Sharp & Co. opened in 2012, when brothers Ray and Tommy Delgado took over an old barber shop on East 6th and fixed it up themselves — new chairs, same brick walls, same smell of talc and aftershave.

Not much has changed since, and that is the point. You sit down, you get a proper cut, a hot towel, and a bit of conversation if you want it. Most of our customers have been coming for years. Some now bring their kids — the same kids who had their first cut in the booster seat by the window.
